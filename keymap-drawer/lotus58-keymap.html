Lotus58 keymap

Drawn from config/lotus58.keymap on the Lily58 column-stagger layout, plus the two encoder push keys between the halves. Pick a layer (arrow keys also work), or click a layer key on the board to jump to it. Hover any key to see what it does on every layer.

Key inspector

Hover or tap a key on the board.

Notes

  • Small blue text is the hold action (home-row mods, balanced flavor, 200 ms tapping term).
  • ▽ means transparent: the key falls through to the layer below.
  • The shaded key is the one held to reach the layer you're viewing.
  • Encoders: left turns volume; right turns Page Up/Down (on Lower it's next/previous track).
  • Adjust is Lower + Raise held together. Its keys 1–5 pick Bluetooth profiles 1–5.
  • Each profile (and USB) remembers its own Mac or Win mode. Lower + Raise + A or ; (the OS keys) flips the current one, so the right mods come back whenever you switch to that profile. The nice!view shows MAC or WIN under the layer name.
  • Lower + Raise + D or K (Anim) turns the nice!view animation on or off. When on, it covers the top of the screen 5 seconds after power-on, hides while Adjust is held and comes back 5 seconds after Adjust is released. The layer name below it stays visible.
  • The mode is written to flash 60 seconds after you press OS. Wait a minute before turning the keyboard off, or the change is lost.
  • In Win mode, Ctrl + Tab (hold K or D, like Cmd+Tab on the Mac) opens the Alt+Tab app switcher. Keep holding and tap Tab to move along, add Shift to go back, and let go to pick the app. Ctrl + Q sends Alt+F4 to close the app, like Cmd+Q.
  • Num: hold the right outer thumb for a left-hand numpad (W E R = 7 8 9, S D F = 4 5 6, X C V = 1 2 3; Space = 0, Lower = ., Delete = -). Shift + digit gives the usual symbol.
  • Mouse: hold the left outer thumb. H J K L move the pointer and M , scroll, matching the arrows and Page Down/Up on Lower. Enter, Raise and Backspace click left, right and middle.
